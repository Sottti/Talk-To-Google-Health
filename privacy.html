Privacy Policy — Talk to Google Health

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Talk to Google Health is a personal integration built and used solely by Pablo Costa Tirado. It is not a public service.

What data is handled

When Pablo dictates what he ate or drank to his personal AI assistant, the assistant looks the food up in Google's food database (or estimates values for custom items) and writes a nutrition or hydration log entry into his own Google Health account via the Google Health API, using Google OAuth credentials he granted himself.

Where data goes

Log entries are stored in his Google Health account and are visible in his Google Health app. No data is sent to, shared with, or sold to any third party. No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used.

Data retention and deletion

Entries live in his Google Health account under his control and can be deleted there at any time. Revoking the app's access in his Google Account settings immediately stops any further writes.
